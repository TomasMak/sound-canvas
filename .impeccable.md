## Design Context

### Users
Music fans and creators using the product to turn songs into visual art. They want the experience to feel curated, gallery-like, and easy to explore without looking noisy or overly technical.

### Brand Personality
Modern, minimal, refined.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use the reference direction from catalystbehavioral.com: calm luxury palette, soft editorial spacing, elegant typography, and composed visual rhythm. Adapt that mood to a music-art product with sharper borders, cleaner structure, and restrained motion. The interface should feel immersive and premium rather than playful or loud.

### Design Principles
- Use calm, minimal colors with warm neutrals and deep ink contrast.
- Favor sharper borders and deliberate structure over soft glassy cards.
- Create an editorial layout with strong whitespace and clear reading rhythm.
- Use motion sparingly and purposefully, especially on scroll and reveal transitions.
- Keep the experience polished and contemporary for both listeners and creators.
